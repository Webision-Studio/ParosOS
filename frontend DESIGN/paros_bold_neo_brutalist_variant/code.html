GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you switch your cafe to Paros.

Do UPI payments go directly to my own bank account? expand_more

Yes, 100%. Paros does not hold your funds or act as an intermediary escrow. When guests scan your universal QR code, payments route straight into your existing merchant UPI VPA or bank QR. You pay 0% transaction commission to Paros.

What happens if our cafe Wi-Fi goes down during a rush? expand_more

Paros operates as an offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA). You can continue punching orders, assigning tables, and accepting cash or direct UPI. Everything automatically syncs to your cloud dashboard the moment connection restores.

Can I still print paper bills if an older customer insists? expand_more

Absolutely. While Paros is engineered to replace paper with WhatsApp receipts, it also supports standard Bluetooth thermal ESC/POS printers in case an occasional guest specifically requests a printed slip.

How long does cafe menu setup take? expand_more

You can import your existing menu in 180 seconds via Excel/CSV or simply send a photo of your printed menu to our WhatsApp onboarding team. We will structure your items, modifiers, and GST categories for free.
